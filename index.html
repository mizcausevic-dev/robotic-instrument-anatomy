What this is

A 34-second mechanical anatomy of a generic tendon-driven wristed instrument on a generic patient-side arm. Hardware stays in muted metal. Cyan, gold, and violet are diagram colors, not glowing parts.

What this is not

  • Not a proprietary teardown and not a named surgical system.
  • No cable diameters, pulley ratios, encoder counts, or tip force sensing. Those are omitted, not measured as zero.
  • No clinical setup, energy settings, or steps for use on a patient.
  • Shaft roll is a fourth proximal input. It is not animated, so the section plane stays put.

How the model is organized

    Product frame

    1. Product: a frame-locked explainer of the mechanism.
    2. Who it is for: engineers, trainees, and technical readers who need the causal path.
    3. Why it is specific: continuous camera, sectional views, and an explicit refusal of invented specifications.
    4. Structure: five stages, frames 0–149, 150–299, 300–509, 510–749, 750–1019.
    5. Discovery: this page and its repository.
    6. Engagement: play, scrub, or jump stages. Reduced motion stays on the chosen stage.
    7. Conversion: comprehension. There is no lead form.
    8. Money: none on this page. The pattern can be reused for other mechanisms.
    9. Launch: static files. No account, no analytics tag, no secrets.
    10. After launch: change jaw or arm geometry only with a sourced review. Do not add measurements without a source.

    Keys: Space plays or pauses. Arrow keys step one frame. Shift plus an arrow steps one second.
